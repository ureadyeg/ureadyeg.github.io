What is UReady?

UReady is a career development platform in Egypt. It helps students, graduates and professionals grow by connecting them with mentors and experts, workshops, challenges, events and communities for every specialty. Its slogan is "The future belongs to the Ready."

What can you do on UReady?

  • Book one-to-one mentorship and feedback sessions with experts in your field.
  • Join workshops and challenges run by experts and companies.
  • Follow events and join communities by university and industry.
  • Share posts and learn from people at every stage of their journey.

Who is it for?

Anyone in Egypt and the Middle East who wants to grow at any stage of their career, and experts who want to share their experience.

Is UReady free?

Joining UReady is free. Some expert sessions may be paid, and the expert sets the price.
