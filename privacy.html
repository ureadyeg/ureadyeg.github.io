Privacy Policy

UReady ("we") respects your data. This page explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and your rights. We follow the principles of Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

1. What we collect

  • Account: name, email, birth year, country, and your sign-in method. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email.
  • Profile: photo, university and major, job title, company, LinkedIn link, and for mentors their specialty and experience. Your interests, if you choose them.
  • Activity: posts, comments, likes, follows, saved items, community memberships, challenge entries, and how long you spend on posts (used to rank your feed).
  • Bookings and payments: your bookings and registrations, records of payment statements between you and a mentor, and a log of booking steps. We do not collect card or bank details. UReady does not handle payments.
  • Messages you send through our contact form, and the date you accepted these terms.

2. Why we use it

To run your account and profile, personalize your feed, connect learners with mentors, keep booking and payment records to help resolve disputes, review mentors, prevent abuse, and improve the platform. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Who can see it

  • Public on the platform: your name, photo, title, company, posts and the profile details you chose to show.
  • Not public: your email, birth year, interests and activity records. Only you and the UReady administrators can see them. A mentor you book sees what is needed for that booking.
  • Booking and payment records are visible to the two users involved and to administrators.

4. Service providers and where data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase on servers in the European Union. Sign-in with Google, email delivery and website hosting are provided by third-party services that process only what they need to work. This means your data is stored and processed outside Egypt. By creating an account you consent to this transfer. We limit what is shared and use these providers only to run UReady.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's storage only to keep you signed in and remember your language and settings. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we remove your profile and personal data. We keep booking and payment records and the booking log after deletion, because they may be needed to resolve disputes and for legal reasons.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email ureadygrow@gmail.com from your account email. To delete your account, use the subject "Delete my account". We reply within 30 days.

8. Security and children

We use access controls so that each user sees only their own private data. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell affected users and the authorities as the law requires. Please report any problem you notice. UReady is not for anyone under 16.

9. Changes and contact

When this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions: ureadygrow@gmail.com.
